# Power-Bi-Health-care-Analysis-Dashboard

# 👋 Welcome to My Healthcare Dashboard Project!
Hey there! I'm super excited to share this Power BI project where I’ve explored a rich healthcare dataset to uncover meaningful insights. Whether you’re into data analysis or just curious about how hospitals, insurance providers, and patient behaviors are interconnected — you’re in the right place!

## 📁 About the Dataset
The dataset contains 24,490 rows and 19 columns, covering a wide range of healthcare-related details like:

1) Patient Demographics: ID, Gender, Age, and Blood Type

2) Clinical Details: Medical Conditions, Medications, and Test Results

3) Administrative Info: Hospitals, Insurance Providers, and Admission Types

4) Billing & Discharge: Billing Amounts, Discharge Dates (Month/Year)

5) This dataset provides a 360-degree view of patient journeys, hospital performances, and financial flows in the healthcare system. Let’s dive into what I discovered!

# 📊 Dashboard Highlights
## 🟩 Page 1: Financial & Admission Insights
### Highest Billing Hospitals:
Zuniga-White and Zuniga-Lopez top the list with the highest billing amounts (above $50K+), while Zuniga-Mann and Zuniga-Marquez remain on the lower end.

### Insurance Provider Contribution (Waterfall Chart):
Aetna and United Health contribute significantly to total billing (total: 1.42 Billion).

### Admission Type Analysis:
Elective and Urgent admissions show very close billing numbers (~0.48bn each), with Emergency slightly behind.

### Interactive Filters:
Filters allow slicing the data by Age, Gender, and Blood Type for deeper analysis.

# 🟩 Page 2: Medical Conditions & Discharge Trends
Medical Condition Frequency by Insurance:
Highest cases fall under Cigna and Medicare, while Aetna reports the least.

## Test Results vs Medication (Donut Chart):
Test results are almost evenly distributed across Normal, Abnormal, and Inconclusive, each near 33%.

## Discharge Trends (Line & Bar Charts):
Peak discharge observed in May (321 patients) and October (297 patients).
A sudden drop in 2024 suggests possible data lag or change in discharge policy.

# ✅ Key Recommendations
## 1) Improve Underperforming Hospitals:
Zuniga-Mann and Zuniga-Marquez need performance review—possible staffing or patient inflow issues.

## 2) Enhance Insurance Strategies:
Focus on maximizing value from high contributors like Aetna and United Health. Review Cigna’s billing patterns for potential improvements.

## 3) Address Test Result Reliability:
The high number of inconclusive results is a red flag—labs and diagnostic protocols need evaluation.

## 4) Monitor Discharge Irregularities:
The dip in 2024 discharge percentage should be cross-checked—could be due to system errors or real policy shifts.

## 5) Utilize Admission Insights:
Balanced billing across admission types offers flexibility—emergency services might need better financial optimization.

# 📌 Project Summary
In this project, I created an insightful and interactive Power BI dashboard using a healthcare dataset to visualize patterns in billing, insurance, admissions, and discharges. The analysis not only reveals trends but also offers actionable recommendations that could support better decision-making in healthcare management.
